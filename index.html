Features

What is inside.

Every screen below is the real app, filled with made-up data for an imaginary person called Alex.

Today

Your day, in one list.

Habits, basics, routines and tasks for today, in the order you plan to do them.

  • One tap to tick. Each item shows its time and its streak.
  • Plan my day. Give the things you want to do a time, and the rest stays out of the way.
  • Basics ready to go. Everyday things like water, sunlight and movement are there from the start.
  • Goals with an amount for the day, the week or the month, like 100 pages a week.

Habits

Habits that keep their history.

Each habit has its own screen, with the whole year on it.

  • Your schedule. Every day, some days of the week, or a few times a week.
  • A square per day. Days done, the share of planned days and your best streak, year by year.
  • Fix a day you forgot. Log today or go back, with an optional note.
  • Reminders for each habit, at the time you choose.

Training

Every kind of training.

Strength, running, cycling, swimming, sports and more. Log as little or as much as you like.

  • "I trained" counts a workout with one tap. Add the type, distance, time and how it felt if you want.
  • Workout plans and an exercise library with step-by-step instructions.
  • Your records, kept as you go.
  • No double logging. A workout can tick a habit or add to a goal by itself.

Food

Food, macros and vitamins.

A food diary measured against targets you set yourself.

  • Calories, protein, carbs and fat for the day, with what is left to go.
  • Vitamins and minerals, recipes, your own foods and supplements.
  • The week at a glance: food goals, weekly limits and what you ate most.
  • Water in litres and a fasting timer, in the same place.

Sleep and mood

How you slept. How you feel.

Two short check-ins that give the rest of your numbers some context.

  • Mood, one face a day. A daily check-in for mood, energy and stress, with the month at a glance.
  • Sleep. When you went to bed and woke up, how you slept, and a sleep goal of your own.
  • Averages and trends for both, so one bad night does not tell the whole story.
  • Health Connect, if you allow it: FORJA can read your sleep, steps, exercise and weight.

Day by day

Everything you did, in one place.

Open any day and see it whole: routines, training, sleep, focus and reading in one list.

  • Reading. Your books, the pages you read and a goal for the year.
  • Focus timer. Sessions add up by day and by week.
  • Routines. A morning routine or a wind-down, step by step.
  • A note for the day, in a line or two.

Progress and Compare

See what is changing, week by week.

Your own numbers, compared only with your own past.

  • Any period, from 3 days to a year, next to the period before it.
  • By area: training, food, sleep, focus, mood, habits, weight and reading.
  • Compare up to four measures side by side, like sleep and mood. Tap a week to read them all together.
  • Save an image of your progress to share, with only what you choose to show.

Android widgets

On your home screen.

Tick a habit, log a food or check your week without opening the app. Widgets follow the theme you picked.

There are also widgets for your weight, a countdown to a date you starred, your daily basics and a line of your own words. The pictures are the widgets' own previews, drawn with sample data.

Private by design

What you log stays on your phone. Nowhere else.

A habit app ends up knowing a lot about you: what you eat, how you sleep, how you feel. FORJA is built so that only you can see it.

Read the privacy policy

  • No account

    FORJA works without signing up. There is no email to give and no password to lose.

  • Kept only on your phone

    Everything you enter is saved in the app's own storage. FORJA has no servers that receive it, and we cannot see it.

  • No ads, no analytics

    Nothing in the app tracks what you do or builds a profile of you.

  • No cloud copy

    Android's automatic backup to your Google account is switched off for FORJA. To keep a copy or move to a new phone, you export a backup yourself.

  • Nothing leaves unless you send it

    A backup you export or a progress image you share goes where you choose, through Android's share menu.

  • A lock on the door

    Set a PIN or use your fingerprint to stop other people opening FORJA. The lock does not encrypt your data or your exported backups.

Questions

Before you ask.

When can I download FORJA?

Not yet. FORJA is being prepared for a closed test on Google Play, and the public release comes after that. This page will say so when it is available.

Is it free?

The current beta is free, with no ads. If a paid feature is ever added, its price and conditions will be shown before you buy anything.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no login. You open the app and start.

Where is my data, and what happens if I change phone?

It is kept only on your phone, and there is no cloud copy. That also means it is yours to look after: uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

To keep a copy or move to a new phone, use Profile → Export data. Android's share menu opens and you choose where the backup file goes. Keep it somewhere private, because the file is not encrypted.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. FORJA does not send what you log anywhere, so tracking works the same offline.

What does FORJA read from Health Connect?

Only if you choose to connect it: steps, sleep, weight and exercise sessions. FORJA only reads them, to show them in the app and in your own statistics. It never writes to Health Connect, and the readings stay on your phone. You can remove the access at any time in Android's Health Connect settings.

Is FORJA a medical app?

No. FORJA is a personal tracking app. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

Scores, nutrition values and targets are estimates based on what you enter. Nutrition targets are estimates for adults, and fasting is not suitable for everyone. For anything about your health, talk to a qualified professional.

Is there an iPhone version?

No. FORJA is being released for Android.

Which languages does it support?

The app is in English.

How do I get in touch?

Write to forja.app.contact@gmail.com.
