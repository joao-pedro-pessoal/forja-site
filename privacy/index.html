FORJA Privacy Policy

Effective 5 November 2026

In short: FORJA keeps everything you log on your phone. It has no account, no ads and no analytics, and it does not send your data to us or to anyone else.

Who is responsible

FORJA is made by João Novais, in Portugal. For any question about this policy or your data, write to forja.app.contact@gmail.com.

What FORJA stores, and where

Everything you enter is saved only on your phone, in the app's own storage: habits and tasks, goals and plans, workouts, sleep, food and water, supplements, weight and measurements, mood, journal entries and notes, focus sessions, reading, savings goals, people you choose to add, quit trackers, lab results and check-ups, and your settings. FORJA does not have servers that receive this data, and we cannot see it.

If you set a PIN to lock the app, it is kept in Android's secure storage on your phone. Fingerprint or face unlock is handled by Android: FORJA only learns whether the check succeeded.

Health Connect

If you choose to connect Health Connect, FORJA can read your steps, sleep, weight and exercise sessions. It only reads; it never writes to Health Connect.

  • FORJA uses these readings only to show them in the app and to include them in your own statistics.
  • They stay on your phone. They are not sent to us or to anyone else, not used for advertising, and not sold.
  • You can stop at any time in Android's Health Connect settings, where you can also remove FORJA's access.

What leaves your phone

Nothing, unless you decide to send it:

  • Backups. When you export a backup, it is copied to your phone's clipboard, and you choose where to paste it (for example, a note or an email to yourself). Wherever you keep it, that service's own terms apply.
  • Sharing. When you share a progress image, it goes only to the app you pick.
  • Links. Links in the app, such as support helplines, open in your browser. The website you visit receives what any website receives, such as your IP address.

Notifications and widgets

Reminders are created and delivered on your phone; FORJA does not use a push service. Home screen widgets show some of your data on the home screen, where anyone holding your phone can see it. You can remove them at any time.

Permissions

  • Notifications, for the reminders you set.
  • Health Connect (steps, sleep, weight, exercise), only if you connect it.
  • Biometrics, only if you lock the app with your fingerprint or face.
  • Internet, which the app framework declares. With the features in this version, FORJA does not use it to send your data.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. Profile → Delete all data removes everything from the app, and uninstalling FORJA does the same. Backups you exported are kept wherever you saved them, and you can delete them there.

Your rights

Because your data is kept on your device and we do not receive it, you already have it and control it: you can see it, export it, correct it and delete it in the app. If you have questions, write to forja.app.contact@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, CNPD, or to the authority in your country.

Health information

FORJA is a personal tracking app. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition, and it is not a substitute for advice from a health professional.

Children

FORJA is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If a future version starts sending any data off your phone, such as an optional community, we will update this policy and explain the change in the app before it takes effect.
